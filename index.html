Common questions

Will an expired album delete my photos automatically?

No. Expired albums appear in “To clean up”. Deleting or cleaning an album requires your confirmation and deletes its managed originals from the system photo library, including imported items. The operating system may ask for additional confirmation. iCloud Photos may sync deletions to your other devices.

How do I keep photos but remove them from the app?

Use “Remove from App” in the media detail or selection view. This removes app management while keeping originals in the system photo library. To keep managing media without an expiry date, move it to an album set to keep forever.

How do I import a video or screen recording?

Open an album, use its add-media action and choose the import option. Select photos or videos from the system picker. Then select the imported media in the compression or watermark tool. Text watermarks currently support still images.

Why is a photo or video unavailable?

Check photo permissions in system Settings and make sure the original is available locally in Photos. For cloud-only items, first download the original using Photos. If the app reports a failed save, keep the app installed and use its retry action to protect the recovery copy.

What does screenshot management do?

It is off by default. When enabled with full photo access, it adds new screenshots taken after activation to managed albums when the app opens or returns to the foreground. Turning it off stops new imports; existing managed items retain their expiry dates. Expiry still requires confirmed cleanup.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language. Choose the system language or one of the 12 supported languages. System permission prompts and share sheets follow your device settings.

iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 15 or later. App availability depends on release status and region.

相册到期后会自动删除吗？

不会。到期相册进入“待清理”，需经你确认才会清理。删除或清理相册会删除其管理的系统相册原件，包括导入的照片和视频；系统可能再次要求确认。开启 iCloud 照片时，删除可能同步到其他设备。

只想移出 App，保留手机照片怎么办？

在详情或多选界面使用“移出 App”，即可解除管理并保留系统相册原件。若希望继续管理且不过期，可移入设置为永久保留的自建相册。

如何导入视频或录屏？

进入相册，点击添加照片入口，选择从本机相册导入；系统选择器支持图片和视频。导入后可在视频压缩工具中选择。文字水印目前支持静态图片。

为什么看不到原图，或保存失败？

请在系统设置中检查照片权限，并确认原件已下载到系统相册。仅在 iCloud 中的原件，请先通过系统相册下载。如果 App 显示保存失败，请保留 App 并使用重试保存，避免丢失恢复副本。

截图管理会做什么？

此功能默认关闭。主动开启并授予完整照片权限后，App 会在打开或回到前台时纳入开启后的新增截图。关闭后停止纳入，已有截图保留原期限；过期仍需确认清理。

如何切换语言？

打开“设置 → 语言”，可跟随系统或从 12 种语言中选择。系统权限弹窗与分享面板的语言由设备设置决定。
