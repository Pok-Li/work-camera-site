Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Applies to Work Camera 0.4.0

Work Camera is an offline tool. It does not connect to the internet, needs no account, and collects no personal information.

1. We collect nothing

The app has no servers and no ads, analytics or tracking. Your photos, videos and usage are never sent to anyone. Capturing, importing, compressing, watermarking and album management all happen on your device.

2. How permissions are used

  • Camera: Takes photos and videos.
  • Microphone: Records video sound.
  • Photos: Saves, reads and manages your photos and videos. Originals are deleted only after you confirm.

You can turn permissions off in system settings at any time; the related features will then be unavailable.

3. Where your data is stored

Photos and videos are saved to the system photo library. Albums, retention periods and settings are stored only on this device. Uninstalling the app removes these local records; photos and videos in the system library are not affected. System sync such as iCloud Photos follows your system settings.

4. Sharing

Files leave the app only when you share them through the system share sheet. They are then handled by the app you choose.

5. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, with no analytics scripts or cookies. GitHub may record visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See its privacy statement.

6. Contact

Questions? Email pok.li@icloud.com. Your email is used only to reply to you. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
