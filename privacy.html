Privacy policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Applies to Work Camera 0.4.0

Work Camera (工作相机) is developed by Pok Li. This policy explains how the app handles your information, and how information is handled when you contact support or visit this website.

1. Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account. It processes photos, videos, audio recorded with videos, thumbnails, media identifiers and metadata on your device to provide capture, import, playback, compression, watermarks, sharing and album management. Album names, colors, retention dates, watermark text, settings and save-recovery records are stored locally.

We do not operate an app backend that receives your media or app usage data. The current app contains no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics service. We do not sell your personal information or use your media to train AI models.

2. Permissions and their purposes

  • Camera: to take photos and record videos.
  • Microphone: to record sound in videos.
  • Photos / media library: to select, display, save and manage photos and videos, including deleting managed originals after confirmation.
  • Full photo access for optional screenshot management: to discover new screenshots after you enable this feature. It is off by default and scanning occurs when the app opens or returns to the foreground.

You can change permissions in system Settings. Denying or limiting a permission may make the corresponding feature unavailable. The app does not request device location permission; media you import or share may already contain metadata supplied by its source.

3. Storage, retention and deletion

Captured media is saved to the system photo library. Importing an existing item registers its original for management rather than creating a second system-library copy. App records and settings remain in the app's local storage. Local recovery copies protect media whose save has not completed; they remain until a successful save or a user action removes them. Temporary sharing copies are eligible for cleanup on a later app launch once they are at least 24 hours old; the operating system may also clear temporary storage.

An expiry date is a cleanup reminder, not automatic deletion. Confirming album cleanup or deletion removes its managed originals from the system photo library, including imported media. “Remove from App” instead keeps the system-library originals and removes app management.

Deleting the app removes its local management records and may remove recovery copies. System-library media may remain. Any system recovery period, device backup or iCloud Photos synchronization follows your operating system and account settings. We cannot access or restore your local media.

4. Sharing, cloud services and other providers

Sharing is initiated by you through the operating system's share sheet. The selected media is provided to your chosen destination, which handles it under its own policies. Copies already shared are not controlled by the app's retention dates.

The app does not provide its own cloud backup or sync service. Device backups and system photo synchronization, such as iCloud Photos, are controlled by your system settings and the provider's policies. A deletion in the system photo library may therefore propagate to other devices. Cloud-only originals may need to be downloaded in Photos before use.

5. Contacting support

If you email pok.li@icloud.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the reported problem and handle your request. Email is processed by the relevant email providers, including iCloud Mail. Please share only what is needed and avoid sending sensitive media.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary follow-up records, or as required by applicable law. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided to support. We do not have a server copy of your app library.

6. This support website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and handles hosting data according to its privacy statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's local media processing.

7. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer, Pok Li, at pok.li@icloud.com.
